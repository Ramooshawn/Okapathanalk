1. Paste your text

One transaction per line works best. The parser looks for a date first, then the description, then the final three numeric values as payment, receipt and balance.
Important: Detected financial figures are kept as entered. The app can show the gap to a target, but it does not silently alter accounting records to manufacture a target profit. Any legitimate adjustment should be entered transparently in the editable table.
